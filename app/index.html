The Public Square
Stakeholders, customers, and voters exist in a public square where ideas are exchanged, credibility is won or lost, and value is created, shared, or squandered. Our stakeholder or grassroots management system maps actors by sentiment, our economic exposure, their policy position, and the movement of the relationship over time.
